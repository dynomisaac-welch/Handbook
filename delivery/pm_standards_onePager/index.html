    PM Standards

    One-Page Quick Reference

    The Five Standards

    1. Status Visibility — No surprises. Weekly written update in Notion every Friday. Health changes get a Teams message within 24h.
    2. Information Stewardship — One place per type. Meetings in Notion, work in ADO, files in SharePoint, decisions logged.
    3. Client Communication — Weekly written update to client. Recaps within 24h. Bad news with a plan, before they ask.
    4. Scope & Risk Management — Risk register weekly. Scope changes via memo with impact + recommendation. No verbal yeses.
    5. Delivery Discipline — Run the ceremonies. Watch the margins. End engagements cleanly.

    Cadence

    • Daily — ADO updates current
    • Weekly Friday — status to Isaac and client
    • Sprint — planning, review, retro
    • Monthly — margin + health review
    • Quarterly — client business review

    Escalate to Isaac immediately when

    • Timeline >10% over OR progress <80% planned
    • Margin within 5 points of target
    • Client escalates above the PM
    • Resource constraint affects delivery
    • Scope change request >10% of effort

    Format: situation, impact, what you're doing, what you need.
